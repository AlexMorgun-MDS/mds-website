AI Email Reply Generator

Draft high-quality email replies in Salesforce in seconds, in the tone you choose.

AI Email Reply Generator is a 100% native Salesforce Lightning app. It reads the email thread on a record, generates a reply draft with the AI provider of your choice, and lets your team review, edit and send it without leaving Lightning Experience.

The app uses a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model: you connect your own AI provider account, and the request goes from your Salesforce org directly to that provider. MDS LLC runs no servers and never receives your data.
